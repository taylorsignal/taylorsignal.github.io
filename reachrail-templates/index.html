Reach Rail — template options

Three directions built from the DailyUI + Abstract Lab formula: logo, one headline, one proof sentence, one CTA, simple form.

01Signal — dark split hero

DailyUI-style: reply-notification graphic on the left, headline + CTA on the right. Navy background, stat strip, centered form.

02Runway — light & centered

Abstract Lab-style: big centered headline, green highlight, trust wordage + placeholder logo strip, side-by-side form section.

2APaper — pure typography

Runway with zero background effects. Bigger type, second line dimmed with a green period, black pill CTA. Benchmark-style restraint.

2BFlightpath — logo motif

Runway with a dotted flight path arcing over the headline, ending in the paper-plane mark. Subtle drift animation.

2CGrid — floating replies

Runway with a faint blueprint grid fading out behind the hero and floating reply chips ("New reply · 2m ago", "Meeting booked").

03Mainline — hybrid editorial

Light page with tilted reply cards, a stats band, a thin 3-step "how it works," and a dark navy form panel with testimonial.

04Inbox Live — dark mission control ⚡

Live inbox with replies streaming in, ticking pipeline counter, logo marquee, animated rail-line process, count-up stats, testimonials with metrics, and an SDR-vs-agency-vs-us comparison table.

05Rail Line — warm & bold ⚡

Clay-energy: cream background, oversized type with rotating serif word, fanned reply cards that spread on hover, tilted stats ticker, bento feature grid, and a full-bleed testimonial band.

5ALetter — the copy brief, verbatim ★

The approved structure: headline + body + qualifier + book-a-call form. One screen. Approved "9 million cold emails" headline, Nasdaq/Deloitte names, nothing else.

5BReceipts — giant proof stat ★

Coldoutbound-style: "9,000,000" counts up huge in the hero, dark tilted stats band with customer names, 3-card offer breakdown + proof-of-concept note, founder call form.

5CFull — Rail Line with real copy ★

Template 5's full layout with the Newslever story everywhere: real headline, real numbers in the ticker and receipts, Nasdaq/Deloitte strip, $3,500/mo qualifier, "Book a 15-minute call" CTAs.

5DNightline — Rail Line, dark mode ◆

Same page, inverted: deep ink background, green ticker band, cream inversion cells, and a cream founder band. Balanced copy (no 9M headline).

5FSticker — bold, dialed back ◆

The playful direction, refined: thinner ink borders, smaller offset shadows, softened pastels, highlighter-block headline kept, most rotations removed. Buttons still press down on click.
